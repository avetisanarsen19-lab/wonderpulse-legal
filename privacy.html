Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Wonder Pulse Poster is a private tool used by its owner to upload original videos to the owner's own TikTok account via the official TikTok Content Posting API.

Data

The app stores a single OAuth access token for the owner's own TikTok account on the owner's local computer. It collects, stores, shares, or sells no personal data of any user, and there are no third-party users of the app.

Third parties

The only third-party service involved is TikTok itself, under TikTok's own Privacy Policy.
